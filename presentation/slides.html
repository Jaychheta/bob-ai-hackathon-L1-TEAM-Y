Architecture & Innovation

The PortFlow AI Solution

An end-to-end mission-control cockpit uniting prediction, optimization, and generative agent assistance.

1. Multi-Horizon Forecasting

Statistical ML models projecting quayside congestion trajectories across 6h, 12h, 24h, 48h, and 72h with spatial hotspot risk scoring.

2. OR-Tools Optimization

Constraint programming (CP-SAT) engine resolving vessel-to-berth and crane assignments to minimize dwell and demurrage.

3. IBM Bob RAG Copilot

Specialized maritime domain AI assistant grounded in live terminal telemetry vectors to generate 1-click recovery directives.

Solution Architecture Slide 3 / 6
Operational Experience

60 FPS Canvas Digital Twin

Ultra-responsive geospatial visualization of quayside berths, gantry cranes, container blocks, and anchorage basins.

Interactive GIS Layout

Vector-drawn terminal geometry rendering Berths B1–B4, Quay Cranes QC-01–QC-08, Container Yards A–D, and outer anchorage zones with zero layout thrashing.

Includes vessel AIS search, dynamic status filtering (Waiting, Berthed, In-Transit), and slide-over entity inspector drawer.

Anti-Jank Performance Architecture

Built strictly following GPU layer promotion (`translateZ(0)`), subpixel typography antialiasing, and composite-only transitions (`opacity`, `transform`).

Instantaneous UI responsiveness even under high-density spatial entity loads.
